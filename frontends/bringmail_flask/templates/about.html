BringMail

BringMail is a local IMAP mail reader with a three-panel layout and an offline cache. All messages are downloaded into a local SQLite database, so folders you have already synced can be browsed without a network connection.

Every account has its own refresh interval. The left panel lists accounts and folders. The other two panels list messages and show the selected message.

HTML mail is sanitized and displayed inside a sandboxed iframe. Remote images are blocked until you click "Load images".

Themes:

Turquoise | Pearl | Slate | Onyx | Default

Layout:

LTB — Left Top Bottom | LMR — Left Middle Right
